## Scope

Public site of Ajaklah News: front page (`public/index.php`), article pages (`public/article.php`), 404, and the share bar component. Mode: **Read** (readers skim, read, then share). Reviewers judge it on desktop; readers mostly on phones.

## Job

Read a story comfortably, then share it in one tap via Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Telegram or Email; the share count updates live. All five buttons always visible; links work without JS; share.js stays ES5.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The news day as a lighting plot on a seamless cyclorama. Pages rise from blackout through a cobalt horizon and rose dawn into white day, so reading happens in daylight and sharing is the cue that brings a story up. It refuses the cream broadsheet front page and the card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cyclorama black #050508 stage, one low cobalt #0A33FF horizon band, rose #FF6AAE gathering above it, dawn rose #FFC1D6, day wash #F7F5FF, white day #FFFFFF. Big Shoulders Stencil Display caps for display and cue labels; Albert Sans for prose and UI, tabular numerals for counts and times. No boxes on the stage: grouping by horizon bands and hairline rules. Platform brand colours act as lighting gels on share controls.

STORY: Readers see the lead story lit on the horizon, scan the rest as a cue sheet (time, title, section, shares), read on a white-day sheet, and fire a share cue that visibly lands.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cyc gradient: black top, cobalt horizon line at ~70% height, rose glow above it. Left: lead headline in stencil caps at display scale, standfirst, byline/time and share count beside it, "Read the story" control. Right: the cover's silhouetted set piece standing on the horizon line. Masthead: wordmark left, date right, hairline rule.

FORM: Cyclorama Dawn (stagecraft-theater-lighting-cyclorama-dawn), user-adopted challenger from the original hand; seed key c25325e1.

SIGNATURE: The scroll is the dawn. The article's fixed cyc moves from night to day as the reader descends into the white-day reading sheet. A share fires a GO cue: the button's gel blooms up the horizon band and the counter steps (snap, no tween). Reduced motion parks every state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
